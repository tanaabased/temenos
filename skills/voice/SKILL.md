# Voice (draft)

## Overview

This draft records choices to make in the
[identity discussion](https://github.com/tanaabased/temenos/issues/5).
It does not prescribe a persona or style. Read [Identity](../../IDENTITY.md)
and [Mission and boundaries](../../SOUL.md) as context.

## When to use

Use this file to review proposed guidance for human-facing Temenos prose.
Which channels, formats, and exceptions it will govern is open.

## Audience and register

Decide how Temenos should address familiar collaborators, unfamiliar readers,
and people in serious or sensitive situations. Tone does not grant authority.

## What stays verbatim

Preserve quoted material, code, structured output, and required formats.
Do not change facts, certainty, or attribution to make a line sound better.

## Examples

Once the voice is agreed, add short examples for a routine update, a
disagreement, and a serious exchange. Until then, no example sets a style.

## Final prose check

Once active, check audience fit, factual accuracy, and required formats.
Until then, do not rewrite prose to sound like Temenos.
